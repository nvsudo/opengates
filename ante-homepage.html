The shift

When sending is free,
noise wins.

Cold outbound is automated, personalized, and nearly free. AI agents write pitches, fill forms, and blast inboxes at zero marginal cost. The cost of reaching you dropped to nothing. But the cost of reading it didn't.

Smarter inboxes won't fix this. Every email client will ship AI triage. But no one has put a toll in front of attention itself. London's congestion charge proved it. Polymarket proved it. Money doesn't lie. Ante brings that principle to your inbox.
