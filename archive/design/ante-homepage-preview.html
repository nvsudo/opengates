Outbound is free.
Your inbox pays the price.
Agents write a million emails before breakfast. The economics of attention are broken. Ante is the fix — a front door with judgment behind it, tone that sounds like you, and criteria that stay hidden.
How it works
Three steps. You're live.
1
Define what you care about.
A few files. What topics matter, what quality looks like, how you sound when you say no. Chat with an LLM, drop the files, you're live.
2
Senders make their case.
They find your gate page, pick a topic, and write. The gate reads it, decides if it needs more, and asks exactly one follow-up at a time. Bounded. Direct.
3
You see only what's worth your time.
Declined warmly. Clarified efficiently. Escalated with a clean summary. You decide what happens next.
Opinions are cheap until they're on Polymarket.
When anyone can send anything to anyone for free, noise wins. Ante changes the economics. Senders put something on the table — effort, specificity, or money — and a gate with real judgment decides what gets through. Your criteria stay hidden. The sender just experiences a conversation that's direct, fair, and fast.
Open-source core. Inspect everything.
Ante is powered by OpenGates, an open-source runtime you can self-host, audit, and extend. Your gate logic, your data, your rules. The hosted product adds convenience — not lock-in.
