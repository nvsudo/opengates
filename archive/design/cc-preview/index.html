The shift

When anyone can send anything to anyone for free, noise wins.

Cold outbound is now automated, personalized, and nearly free. The cost of reaching someone dropped to zero. But the cost of reading it didn't. Attention is the last scarce resource — and it has no price signal.

Ante creates that signal. Senders put something on the table — effort, specificity, sometimes money — and a gate with real judgment decides what gets through. Your criteria stay hidden. The sender just experiences a conversation that's direct, fair, and fast.

How it works

Three files. One URL. You're live.

A gate is a few markdown files that describe what you care about, how you sound, and what good looks like. No dashboards. No integrations. Just intent, expressed clearly.

1
Define your criteria
Write what matters in plain language. Topics you're interested in. Standards you won't compromise on. The tone you use when you say no. Drop a few markdown files — you're live.
2
Senders make their case
They hit your gate URL, pick a topic, and write. The gate reads it, decides if it needs more, and asks exactly one follow-up at a time. Bounded. Direct. No runaround.
3
You see only what survives
Declined warmly. Clarified efficiently. Escalated with a clean summary. You touch nothing until something actually deserves your attention.

It's a conversation,
not a form.

Most filters are binary — pass or block. Ante runs a bounded conversation. It reads the pitch, asks a sharp follow-up, and decides based on what comes back. The sender gets a fair shot. You get signal, not slush.

Every gate speaks in your voice. Declines are warm, not corporate. Follow-ups are specific, not templated. The person on the other end never knows the criteria — they just feel like they're talking to someone sharp who respects their time.

Under the hood

A gate is just a few files.

No dashboard. No drag-and-drop builder. A gate is markdown that describes what you care about, written the way you think. Focus areas. Quality standards. Voice and tone. Example conversations. That's it.

focus.md What topics and signals matter to you
standards.md Your quality bar — specificity, traction, fit
voice.md How the gate sounds — warm, direct, you

Your rules

You choose the economics.

Some gates are free — effort is the filter. Some charge a flat fee to cover tokens and weed out noise. Some run priority lanes. You decide what's right for your audience.

🆓
Free
Effort and specificity are the only filters. The gate's judgment does the work.
🎫
Flat fee
$5, $50, your call. Covers token cost and signals seriousness. Not a paywall — a noise floor.
⚡
Priority lane
Free path stays open. Paid path gets faster processing and priority review.
📈
Auction
Senders set their price. Market-driven signal for high-demand principals.
